A Child’s Idea of Rescue

A Child’s Idea of Rescue explores the fragile relationship between safety, trust, and the expectations we place upon the things we believe will protect us.

Drawing upon the familiar form of a life preserver, an object designed to offer security in moments of vulnerability, the work presents a contradiction between the promise of protection and the reality of its limitations. Its recognizable, playful imagery evokes a sense of innocence and familiarity while quietly questioning the reliability of the structures we depend upon.
